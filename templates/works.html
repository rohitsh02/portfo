AI Energy Advisor

LangGraphGeminiCopilotKit SupermemoryCloud Run

A stateful multi-agent system built on LangGraph state machines, with tool-calling and memory that survives between sessions. It fields live tariff questions, sizes solar, and finds demand-charge savings — all grounded on the user's own billing data, so it can't invent numbers. The interesting problem here wasn't the model, it was making it refuse to guess.

live

Bill Intelligence Pipeline

FastAPIPythonMongoDB Atlas n8nWhatsApp API

Watches a Gmail inbox, pulls the data out of MSEDCL bill PDFs in Marathi and English, runs it through an LLM, flags anything that looks wrong, and pushes it to the consumer on WhatsApp. 500+ bills a month with nobody in the loop. Bilingual PDF extraction was, predictably, the part that ate the most time.

Volt Platform

FastAPIGeminiMongoDB GCPReact

A full energy SaaS: 10+ analytics APIs, a six-tab React dashboard, and LLM-generated commentary on every report — the sentence a human analyst would have written under the chart, without the human. Includes BESS and solar ROI modelling that had to match the finance team's spreadsheet exactly, which it does.

live

Grid IoT Monitoring

MQTTModbusWebSockets TimescaleDBRaspberry PiFastAPI

Raspberry Pis sitting in electrical rooms, reading meters and inverters over Modbus/RS485, publishing over MQTT, streaming into a FastAPI server over WebSockets, and landing in TimescaleDB — with anomaly alerts firing to consumers in real time. This is the one where the electrical engineering degree finally earned its keep.
