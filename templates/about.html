about me

I started with circuits, ended up with agents.

I'm an AI engineer based in Nagpur. I've spent the last three years at Grid Insight building LLM-powered systems for the energy sector — agents, RAG pipelines, REST APIs, and all the infrastructure underneath that nobody puts on a slide.

My degree is in Electrical & Electronics Engineering. I did not plan on any of this. I got curious about machine learning somewhere around 2021, taught myself Python properly, and then spent an embarrassing number of nights on ML courses and side projects until the notebooks turned into something people actually paid for.

The thing that stuck with me from the electrical side is that a wrong number has consequences. When you're telling someone what their electricity bill should be, or whether a solar install pays for itself, "the model said so" is not an acceptable answer. So most of my work is about the boring, load-bearing parts: schema-enforced LLM outputs, fallback chains, grounding answers in the user's real data, and making sure the whole thing doesn't fall over at 3am.

These days that means LangGraph and Gemini on the agent side, FastAPI and Docker on Cloud Run for the backend, n8n for the glue, and — pleasingly — Raspberry Pis wired into actual meters, which is the closest my degree and my job have ever come to shaking hands.
